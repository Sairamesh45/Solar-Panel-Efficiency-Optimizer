## Step-by-Step Usage

### 1. Generate Analytics

1. Select your solar panel
2. Choose date range (default: last 30 days)
3. Enter system cost (optional, for ROI calculation)
4. Set electricity tariff (default: ₹6.5/kWh)
5. Click **🔄 Refresh**

### 2. View Your Metrics

- **Financial**: See savings, ROI, payback period
- **Environmental**: Check CO₂ avoided and equivalents
- **All-Time**: View cumulative statistics

### 3. Download Reports

1. Choose report type (PDF or Excel)
2. Set date range
3. Enter parameters
4. Click **⬇️ Download Report**
5. File downloads automatically!

## Understanding the Metrics

### Financial Metrics Explained

**Total Savings**

- Formula: Energy Produced × Electricity Tariff
- What it means: Money saved on electricity bills

**Net Savings**

- Formula: Total Savings - Maintenance Costs
- What it means: Actual profit after expenses

**ROI (Return on Investment)**

- Formula: (Net Savings / System Cost) × 100
- What it means: Percentage return on your investment

**Payback Period**

- Formula: System Cost / Monthly Savings
- What it means: Months until you recover investment

### Environmental Metrics Explained

**CO₂ Avoided**

- Formula: Energy Produced × 0.82 kg/kWh
- What it means: Carbon emissions prevented

**Trees Equivalent**

- Formula: CO₂ Avoided / 21.77 kg per tree/year
- What it means: Trees needed to absorb same CO₂

**Cars Off Road**

- Formula: (CO₂ Avoided / 4.6 tons) × 365
- What it means: Days of average car emissions offset

## Troubleshooting

**No Data Showing?**

- Make sure you have sensor data for the selected period
- Check that your panel has performance records
- Try selecting a different date range

**Reports Not Downloading?**

- Check browser popup blocker settings
- Ensure panel is selected
- Verify date range is valid

**Inaccurate Calculations?**

- Verify electricity tariff matches your actual rate
- Double-check system cost entry
- Ensure sensor data is being recorded properly
